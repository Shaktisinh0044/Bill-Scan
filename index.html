About us

We watched hours disappear into typing bills.

Every evening, thousands of shop owners and traders across India sit down with a notebook full of handwritten bills — and type them, line by line, into billing software. It takes hours. Mistakes creep in. And the work never ends.

We saw it happen in our own family business circles in Ahmedabad. The handwriting was already there. The data was already there. Only the typing stood in between.

So we built BillScan — point your camera at the page, and the bill is done.
