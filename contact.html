Quick answers

Is BillScan free to use?

Right now BillScan is in its early access phase with our first group of users. Message us on WhatsApp to get access.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — BillScan works in any browser on any phone. No app install needed. Take a photo, upload, done.

What if it reads something wrong?

Everything is editable before you download. BillScan also matches items against a product list of 666+ parts to auto-correct names and find the right MRP and discount.

Can it use my own bill format?

Yes — bills print in the Mama (M-143 Estimate) format, and more formats are coming. Message us if you need your specific format added.
