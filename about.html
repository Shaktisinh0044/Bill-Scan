Our story

We watched hours disappear
into typing bills.

BillScan started with one simple observation in an Ahmedabad auto parts market — and one question: why is anyone still typing this by hand?

Why we built this

Every evening, thousands of shop owners and traders across India sit down with a notebook full of handwritten bills — and type them, line by line, into billing software. It takes hours. Mistakes creep in. And the work never ends.

We saw it happen in our own family business circles in Ahmedabad. The handwriting was already there. The data was already there. Only the typing stood in between.

So we built BillScan — point your camera at the page, and the bill is done. Item names, quantities, rates, discounts, GST — read automatically, matched against your own product list, and printed in your own bill format.
